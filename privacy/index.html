Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. Learn how we handle your personal information and safeguard your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

At Mombasa Boxing Community (MBC), we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when interacting with our site, submitting forms, or reaching out to us.

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, and phone number when submitting inquiries or registration forms.
  • Inquiry Details: Specific messages regarding ticket reservations, fighter registration, or partnership proposals.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser types, IP addresses, and page navigation statistics gathered via standard web logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to improve community engagement, manage events, and communicate with you:

  • To respond to your inquiries submitted via our contact forms.
  • To process athlete registrations and event ticket reservations.
  • To send important updates regarding fight cards, venue adjustments, or schedule changes.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party providers to enhance user experience and facilitate form processing:

  • Formspree: Our contact form submissions are processed through Formspree. Information submitted is subject to Formspree’s privacy policy.
  • Embedded Fonts & CDNs: We use Google Fonts and Tailwind CDN for site styling, which may record standard browser request metadata.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you or request its deletion. If you have any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us through our Contact Page.
