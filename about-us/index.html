Our Story

For the Love of The Game

Mombasa Boxing Community (MBC) is dedicated to elevating boxing in the coastal region. We build champions inside the ring and leaders outside it.

Our Mission

To scout, nurture, and showcase raw boxing talent across Mombasa and the broader coastal strip. We aim to provide structured opportunities, high-caliber fight events, and professional pathway infrastructure for young boxers to thrive internationally.

Our Vision

To transform Mombasa into East Africa’s premier boxing hub. By blending coastal heritage with world-class promotional standards, we inspire community pride and unite people under one banner: One Belt. One City. One Champion.

What We Stand For

Knockouts & Rivalries

Delivering unforgettable fight nights and fierce, competitive match-ups that push athletes to their physical limits.

Youth Empowerment

Channeling energy off the streets into disciplined training routines that build character, focus, and resilience.

Community & Pride

Bringing together neighborhoods, families, and fans across iconic local venues like Mama Ngina Waterfront.

Mombasa Boxing Community

Empowering local athletes and putting coastal boxing on the map.
